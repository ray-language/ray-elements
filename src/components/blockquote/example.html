Why the web components are form-associated

Every field of ray-ds takes part in its <form> like a native control: it is submitted under its name, validated, reset and disabled by a fieldset.

Form-associated custom elements enable web authors to define and create custom elements which participate in form submission.

That is why a FormData built from a form full of ray-ds fields has every value in it, with no glue code.
